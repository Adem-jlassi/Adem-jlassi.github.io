About

Most of what I've built so far sits in the gap between engineering and analysis: standing up a multi-node Hadoop cluster in Docker, cleaning a quarter of a million messy salary records, tuning a classifier until the recall stops flattering you.

I care about the unglamorous half of the job — notebooks that run end to end, train/test splits that stay honest, and results I can explain to someone who doesn't speak machine learning. Right now that means Spark, PySpark and a lot of time spent reading confusion matrices.

I'm looking for a PFE internship where the data is real and the problems don't have a tutorial. In return: careful work, documented code, and findings you can check.

01 · Internship · Tunisie Telecom

Telecom customer churn prediction with Spark

A distributed ML pipeline in PySpark that flags the customers about to leave. Three classifiers trained on 3,333 customer records, tuned with 3-fold cross-validation and selected on the training folds only — then used to split the base into risk segments, where 15% of customers turned out to hold 84% of all churners.

  • Python
  • PySpark
  • Spark MLlib
  • HDFS
  • pandas
  • seaborn

Model choice never touched the test set — a detail worth defending in a review.
